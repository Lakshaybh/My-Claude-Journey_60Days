AB Talks · 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

From First Prompt to Production Launch

How 60 days of deliberate, escalating AI practice culminated in QueryMind — a live, production-hardened application shipped in a 10-day solo capstone sprint.

Weeks 1-2 · AI Foundations & Effective Prompting
Learning to think in prompts

Understanding how large language models actually work, and moving from vague requests to clear, structured, intent-driven prompts — the foundation every later skill builds on.

Weeks 2-3 · Reasoning & Research Workflows
Using AI to think, not just answer

Building multi-step reasoning workflows and AI-assisted research habits — the discipline that later shaped how QueryMind's own problem was discovered through structured interview questions, not guesswork.

Weeks 3-4 · Automation & Product Thinking
From tasks to systems

Automating repetitive workflows and learning to scope a real product — the exact instinct that shaped QueryMind's deliberate v1.0 cuts (no DAX, no execution, no chat) on Day 51 of the capstone.

Weeks 4-6 · Increasingly Sophisticated AI Projects
Raising the bar project by project

Each build more ambitious than the last, compounding into the confidence to design a full production system: architecture, API contracts, and a database rationale, before a single line of app code was written.

Weeks 6-7 · Multimodal AI & Knowledge Systems
Working across formats and memory

Extending beyond text into structured knowledge and multimodal workflows — groundwork for treating a pasted database schema as structured input an AI could reason over reliably.

Weeks 7-8 · Autonomous Agents & Multi-Agent Collaboration
Directing AI as a working partner

Learning to collaborate with AI as an autonomous, pair-programming partner — precisely the working relationship that carried the entire 10-day capstone, through a real AI-provider pivot, a caught missing feature, and a full UI redesign and reversal.

Days 51-60 · The Capstone
QueryMind — shipped, live, v1.0.0

Every prior skill converged into one real software development lifecycle: discovery → architecture → implementation → a mid-build pivot handled transparently → UI/UX design → security & QA hardening → production deployment. Not a tutorial project — a live tool a stranger can open right now.
