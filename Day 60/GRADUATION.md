# Graduation — QueryMind & the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge

## Graduation Reflection (200 words)

Sixty days ago, this journey started with the fundamentals: understanding how AI actually thinks, learning to prompt with intent instead of guesswork, and building the habit of using AI as a genuine collaborator rather than a search box. Each week raised the bar — research workflows, automation, product thinking, increasingly ambitious builds — until those separate skills had somewhere real to converge.

That place was QueryMind. Ten days, roughly an hour a day, one person and one AI pair programmer, building a live tool from a single honest answer on Day 51 ("writing SQL is the tedious part of my job") through a real mid-build pivot when Claude access hit a genuine wall, through a missing feature caught by careful review instead of a bug report, through a full interface redesign built and then cleanly reversed, through a security and QA audit that found real gaps precisely because nothing was on fire. Every one of those moments was a skill from the earlier 59 days, applied for real, under real constraints, with real consequences if it went wrong.

QueryMind is live. It works. A stranger can open it right now and get a correct SQL query back. That's not a demo — that's the actual outcome of sixty days spent learning to build with AI, not just talk about it.
